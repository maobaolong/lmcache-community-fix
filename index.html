Welcome to the LMCache Community

LMCache is built and maintained by a global community of researchers, systems engineers, and open-source contributors. Together, we improve KV cache reuse, storage, and integration across inference engines to make LLM serving faster and more efficient. Join us in shaping the next generation of inference infrastructure.
